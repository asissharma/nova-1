✍️ Wednesday — Framework
I learned the RICE framework this week.

Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort

Most people lie about "Impact." They score their own idea a 5 because they built it.

The honest way: ask "If this works perfectly, what metric moves and by how much?" If you can't answer, it's not a 5. It's a 2.

I used this on my own side project. My "high impact" feature scored a 2. Brutal. Accurate.

How do you prioritize when everything feels urgent?

🧠 2026 algorithm rules — what actually changed
LinkedIn's feed model now optimizes for depth and dwell time over raw virality, and is noticeably harder on content that looks AI-generated or engagement-baited:
  • Post 2–5×/week, not daily — accounts posting 2+ times a day see reach drop 40%+
  • 2–4 hashtags max — 10+ now cuts reach by roughly a third
  • First 2 lines carry the post — everything after "see more" needs to earn the click
  • Comments of 15+ words carry ~2.5× the algorithmic weight of a short one
  • Reply fast and often — dwell time and conversation depth outweigh likes
  • No engagement pods — coordinated engagement gets detected and penalized
  • Sweet spot for B2B visibility: Tue–Thu, 9–11 AM, first 90 minutes matter most
📝 Content rules
  • Post 3–4×/week (Tue–Fri)
  • 40% learning in public · 30% industry observations · 20% personal narrative · 10% engagement
  • Use line breaks like a copywriter
  • Never put external links in the post body — first comment instead
  • End with a question that drives comments
  • Reply to every comment within 60–90 minutes with a real answer
  • 15 min/day commenting on founders', operators', and VCs' posts
  • 5–10 new connections/week in target roles
